The 24 Hour Track Championship

TWENTY-FOUR

400 meters · 24 hours · real time
The race clock is real and never stops. Close the game and you're sitting in a camp chair while the clock runs.
  • Run: in VR, swing your arms like you're running. On a phone, tap L and R in rhythm (keyboard: F / J). Faster rhythm, faster pace.
  • Don't stop dead. Stop moving your legs at speed and you'll trip. Slow down gradually.
  • Eat ~100 kcal per mile. Drink. Salt. The aid table is on your left just before the finish line. In VR, grip an item and lift it to your mouth. On a phone, tap it as you pass. Slow down or you'll fumble it.
  • Wrist watch (left) shows your race. Body panel (right) shows energy, hydration, stomach, legs, salt and alertness.
Good: 100 miles. Heroic: 200.
